# V441–V455 — IFC concept verification hardening

Five IFC models are reopened and required to have IFC4 project/site/storey, geometry-bearing BIM components, globally unique IDs, and positive architecture/structure/electrical/plumbing category counts. ZIP is atomically published only after the individual models pass validation and exactly five IFC files plus manifest/reference are checked. No claim of reconstruction accuracy, structural engineering, full MEP design or IFC ISO certification. Existing UI controls unchanged.
